THE GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATIONS OF GOODS (REGISTRATION AND PROTECTION) ACT, 1999
CONTROLLER GENERAL OF PATENTS, DESIGNS AND TRADE MARKS (CGPDTM)
GOVERNMENT OF INDIA

GUIDELINES FOR REGISTRATION OF GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATIONS FOR AYURVEDIC & HERBAL PRODUCTS:

1. Definition under Section 2(1)(e):
A Geographical Indication (GI) identifies an agricultural, natural, or manufactured good as originating in a definite territory, region, or locality where a given quality, reputation, or characteristic of the good is essentially attributable to its geographical origin.

2. Eligibility of Ayurvedic & Botanical Medicines:
- Famous herbal origins (e.g., Navara Rice, Malabar Pepper, Alleppey Green Cardamom, Nilambur Teak, Kangra Tea).
- Traditional formulations made exclusively by local tribal and artisan communities with unique climatic/terroir factors.

3. Registration Procedure:
- Step 1: Filing of Application Form GI-1 by an Association of Persons or Producer Organization representing community interests.
- Step 2: Submission of Statement of Case detailing uniqueness, historical evidence, geographical map, and inspection mechanism.
- Step 3: Consultative Expert Committee Examination and Advertisement in GI Journal.
- Step 4: Certificate of Registration (valid for 10 years, renewable indefinitely).

4. Prohibition of Assignment:
- Unlike patents and trademarks, a GI is collective community property and cannot be assigned or licensed to private individuals.
