THE DESIGNS ACT, 2000 (INDIA) — REGISTRATION, LOCARNO CLASSIFICATION & PIRACY OF DESIGNS
Aesthetic Protection of Industrial Products, Exclusions, Term and Infringement Remedies.

1. Definition of Design (Section 2(d)):
"Design means only the features of shape, configuration, pattern, ornament or composition of lines or colours applied to any article whether in two dimensional or three dimensional or in both forms, by any industrial process or means, whether manual, mechanical or chemical, separate or combined, which in the finished article appeal to and are judged solely by the eye."
- Exclusions: Does NOT include any mode or principle of construction or anything which is in substance a mere mechanical device, nor does it include any trade mark or artistic work (as defined in Copyright Act).

2. Prohibition of Registration (Section 4):
A design which:
- (a) is not new or original; or
- (b) has been disclosed to the public anywhere in India or in any other country by prior publication in tangible form or by use; or
- (c) is not significantly distinguishable from known designs or combination of known designs; or
- (d) comprises or contains scandalous or obscene matter,
SHALL NOT be registered.

3. Classification:
- India follows the Locarno Classification (Classes 1 to 32) under the Design Rules, 2001.

4. Term of Copyright in Design (Section 11):
- Initial Registration: Valid for TEN (10) YEARS from the date of registration.
- Extension: Extendable for a second period of FIVE (5) YEARS upon filing Form-3 with fee before expiry of initial 10 years (Maximum total term: 15 years).

5. Piracy of Registered Design (Section 22):
- During the existence of copyright in any design, it shall not be lawful for any person for the purpose of sale to apply the registered design or any fraudulent or obvious imitation thereof to any article in any class in which the design is registered without license or consent of proprietor.
- Remedies:
  a. Statutory Recovery: Sum not exceeding Rs. 50,000 for every contravention recoverable as a contract debt (subject to a maximum of Rs. 1,00,000 for any one design); OR
  b. Injunction & Damages / Account of Profits.
