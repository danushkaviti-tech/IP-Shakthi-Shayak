THE BIOLOGICAL DIVERSITY ACT, 2002 (ACT NO. 18 OF 2003)
NATIONAL BIODIVERSITY AUTHORITY (NBA) • MINISTRY OF ENVIRONMENT, FOREST AND CLIMATE CHANGE

SECTION 6: APPLICATION FOR INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS REQUIRING NBA APPROVAL

1. STATUTORY MANDATE:
- "No person shall apply for any intellectual property right, by whatever name called, in or outside India for any invention based on any research or information on a biological resource obtained from India without obtaining the previous approval of the National Biodiversity Authority before making such application."
- Exemption: Any application for patent made by an Indian applicant under the Protection of Plant Varieties and Farmers' Rights Act, 2001.

2. EXAMINATION & CLEARANCE PROTOCOL:
- When applying for a patent involving Indian biological resources or traditional knowledge, Form III must be filed with the NBA.
- The Patent Office (IPO) withholds the final grant of the patent until the NBA issues an official No Objection Certificate (NOC) and executes an Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS) agreement.

3. CONSEQUENCES OF NON-COMPLIANCE:
- Applications filed without mandatory NBA clearance are liable to rejection/revocation under Section 25 and Section 64 of the Patents Act, 1970.
- Penal sanctions under Section 55 of the Biological Diversity Act include imprisonment up to five years or fine up to ten lakh rupees or both.

Verified by National Biodiversity Authority (NBA) India.
