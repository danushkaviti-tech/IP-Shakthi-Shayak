THE DESIGNS ACT, 2000 (ACT NO. 16 OF 2000)
GOVERNMENT OF INDIA • PATENT OFFICE (DESIGNS WING)

1. DEFINITION OF DESIGN (SECTION 2(d)):
- "Design" means only the features of shape, configuration, pattern, ornament or composition of lines or colors applied to any article whether in two dimensional or three dimensional or in both forms, by any industrial process or means.
- Exclusions: Does not include any mode or principle of construction or anything which is in substance a merely mechanical device.

2. PROHIBITION OF REGISTRATION (SECTION 4):
- A design which is not new or original;
- Has been disclosed to the public anywhere in India or in any other country by prior publication in tangible form or by use or in any other way prior to the filing date;
- Is not significantly distinguishable from known designs or combination of known designs;
- Comprises scandalous or obscene matter.

3. DURATION AND RENEWAL (SECTION 11):
- Initial copyright in registered design: 10 years from the date of registration.
- Extension: Extendable for a second period of 5 years upon payment of prescribed fees before expiration of initial 10-year period. Total duration: 15 years.

4. CLASSIFICATION:
- International Classification for Industrial Designs (Locarno Classification).
