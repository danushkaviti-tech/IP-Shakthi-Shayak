THE COPYRIGHT ACT, 1957 (ACT NO. 14 OF 1957)
AS AMENDED BY THE COPYRIGHT (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2012
GOVERNMENT OF INDIA • COPYRIGHT OFFICE

1. PROTECTED WORKS (SECTION 13):
- Original literary, dramatic, musical, and artistic works.
- Cinematograph films and sound recordings.
- Computer programs, software applications, tables, and databases are protected under 'Literary Works'.

2. RIGHTS OF THE COPYRIGHT OWNER (SECTION 14):
- Exclusive right to reproduce, issue copies, perform in public, make cinematograph film/sound recording, translate, and adapt the work.

3. FAIR DEALING EXEMPTIONS (SECTION 52):
- Fair dealing with any work (not being a computer program) for private or personal use, including research.
- Criticism, review, or reporting of current events and current affairs.
- Educational use by teachers or pupils in the course of instruction.

4. TERM OF COPYRIGHT (SECTIONS 22 - 29):
- Published literary, dramatic, musical, and artistic works: Lifetime of the author plus 60 years from the beginning of the calendar year next following the year in which the author dies.
- Cinematograph films, sound recordings, photographs, and posthumous works: 60 years from the year of publication.

Verified by Copyright Office of India.
