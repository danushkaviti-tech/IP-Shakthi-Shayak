THE PATENTS ACT, 1970 (INDIA) — SECTION 25: PRE-GRANT & POST-GRANT OPPOSITIONS
Statutory Framework for Opposing Patent Applications and Granted Patents.

1. Pre-Grant Opposition (Section 25(1) & Rule 55):
- Who can file: "Any person" (no need to establish commercial or proprietary interest).
- Window to file: From the date of publication of the application under Section 11A until the date of grant of patent under Section 43.
- Mode: Representation by way of opposition in writing, supported by documentary evidence, filed before the Controller of Patents.
- Grounds (Section 25(1)(a)-(k)):
  a. Wrongful obtaining of invention from opponent.
  b. Prior publication in India or abroad prior to priority date.
  c. Prior claiming in an earlier Indian patent application.
  d. Prior public knowledge or prior public use in India.
  e. Obviousness and lack of inventive step over cited prior art.
  f. Subject matter is not an invention or falls under Section 3 or Section 4 exclusions.
  g. Insufficient or unclear description of invention in specification.
  h. Failure to disclose or falsely filing foreign application details under Section 8.
  i. Failure to file complete specification within 12 months in convention application.
  j. Non-disclosure or wrongful mention of geographical origin of biological material.
  k. Anticipation by traditional knowledge (oral or written) anywhere in the world.

2. Post-Grant Opposition (Section 25(2) & Rules 55A to 63):
- Who can file: Only a "Person Interested" (a person engaged in, or in promoting research in, the same field to which the invention relates).
- Window to file: Within ONE YEAR from the date of publication of the grant of patent in the Official Patent Journal.
- Procedure: Notice of opposition on Form 7 with written statement and evidence; constitution of Opposition Board by Controller; recommendation by Board; hearing before Controller.
- Grounds: Identical statutory grounds to Section 25(1)(a)-(k).
