TRADITIONAL KNOWLEDGE DIGITAL LIBRARY (TKDL)
COUNCIL OF SCIENTIFIC & INDUSTRIAL RESEARCH (CSIR) & MINISTRY OF AYUSH
GOVERNMENT OF INDIA

GUIDELINES FOR PRIOR ART SEARCH AND DEFENSIVE PROTECTION OF AYURVEDA KNOWLEDGE:

1. Overview:
The Traditional Knowledge Digital Library (TKDL) is a pioneering Indian digital repository containing over 450,000 formulations from ancient classical texts of Ayurveda, Siddha, Unani, and Sowa-Rigpa translated into five international languages (English, German, French, Japanese, Spanish).

2. Purpose & Legal Standard:
- Serves as non-patent prior art defense against biopiracy and wrongful patenting of traditional Indian medicine abroad (USPTO, EPO, JPO).
- References Sanskrit slokas from classical treatises including Charaka Samhita, Sushruta Samhita, Sarngadhara Samhita, and Bhavaprakasha.

3. Key Examination Provisions for AYUSH Inventions:
- If a medicinal formulation matches a recipe in TKDL, novelty is destroyed under Indian Patents Act Section 2(1)(j).
- If modified, the burden is on the applicant to establish non-obviousness and non-infringement of Section 3(p).

4. Access Protocols:
- Available to international patent offices under non-disclosure access agreements to prevent misappropriation.
- Institutional researchers can request prior art clearance for translational commercialization under AYUSH National Policy.
